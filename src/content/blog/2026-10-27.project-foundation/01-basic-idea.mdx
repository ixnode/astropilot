## From an idea to a working foundation

Sproutsite began with a simple question: how can professional static websites be maintained without hiding content in components? The answer is a clear directory structure built with Markdown, YAML, and Astro Content Collections.

From the first version, pages are assembled from their content directories. `page.yaml` holds HTML metadata, `hero.md` defines a page introduction, and numbered files provide its individual sections.
