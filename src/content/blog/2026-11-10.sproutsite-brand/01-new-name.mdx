## A new name for the road ahead

On **10 November 2026**, the new product name was confirmed: **Sproutsite**. The former AstroPilot name has been retired. What began as a file-based Astro project now continues under its own identity.

Sproutsite connects the idea of a sprout with the website that can grow from it. An initial idea becomes content, content becomes pages, and reusable components provide structure. The name reflects that process without obscuring the technical foundation.

The new domain is **[sproutsite.dev](https://sproutsite.dev)**. It is intended as the central address for the project, its documentation, and component examples. The official spelling is Sproutsite: one word, with a capital S.

The supporting brand message captures the idea: _Plant an idea. Grow your website._
