## A sprout on a clear structure

The new name comes with its own symbol: two green leaves grow from three stacked layers. The sprout represents an idea and its development. The layers represent Markdown files, reusable components, and the website they create.

The colour system connects these two sides. Deep navy creates a calm, clear background. Blue, cyan, and teal add depth to the layers and reflect the technical foundation. Fresh green provides a deliberate accent for growth and development.

The wordmark uses the locally served Nunito typeface. It appears dark on light surfaces and white on dark backgrounds. This keeps the name legible while the multicoloured symbol remains distinctive.

Hero areas also reflect the new identity: organic waves connect blue and teal with a restrained green accent. They give the design a more natural direction while the text area remains calm. The rebrand complements the existing website without reinventing its content or structure.
