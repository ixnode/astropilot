## Content with clear rules

Astro Content Collections validate files during the build. Sproutsite adds small schemas for pages, heroes, sections, and site configuration.

Invalid colour variants, missing titles, and unsupported URLs do not first appear in the browser. The error message points directly to the affected file and field.
