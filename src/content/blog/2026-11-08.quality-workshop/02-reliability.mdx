## Quality is part of the workflow

Sproutsite checks content and TypeScript, builds the static website, and automatically checks generated pages for fundamental accessibility issues.

This does not replace editorial judgement. It does help surface common problems such as invalid content, missing internal destinations, or inaccessible interactions early.
