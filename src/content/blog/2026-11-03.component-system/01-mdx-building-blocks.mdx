## Building blocks instead of one-off solutions

Alongside regular Markdown, Sproutsite deliberately supports MDX. It makes tested building blocks such as columns, cards, buttons, notices, and images available exactly where content needs them.

Components belong to the theme, not the content core. Authors use a small, documented surface, while developers have a clear place for tests and styling.
